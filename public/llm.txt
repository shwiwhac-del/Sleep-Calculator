## Technical & Scientific Specifications

Our models are grounded in established sleep physiology and clinical recommendations from the **National Sleep Foundation (NSF)** and the **American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM)**.

### 1. Sleep Cycle Stages & Duration
- **Cycle Duration**: Average human sleep cycle is approximately **90 minutes** (ranging from 80 to 110 minutes in healthy adults).
- **Stage Progression**: N1 (Light Sleep) $\rightarrow$ N2 (Deeper Light Sleep) $\rightarrow$ N3 (Deep / Slow-Wave Sleep) $\rightarrow$ REM (Rapid Eye Movement).
- **Sleep Inertia Prevention**: Waking up during N1 or REM stages leads to feeling refreshed. Waking up during N3 (deep sleep) triggers high sleep inertia, causing grogginess that can last up to 4 hours.

### 2. Sleep Duration Recommendations by Age
- **Toddlers (1–2 years)**: 11 to 14 hours per day.
- **Preschoolers (3–5 years)**: 10 to 13 hours per day.
- **School-Age Children (6–13 years)**: 9 to 11 hours per day.
- **Teenagers (14–17 years)**: 8 to 10 hours per day.
- **Adults (18–64 years)**: 7 to 9 hours per day.
- **Older Adults (65+ years)**: 7 to 8 hours per day.

## Key Q&A & Semantic Answers (GEO-Optimized)

### How do you calculate sleep cycles?
To find the perfect bedtime:
1. Take your desired **wake-up time**.
2. Count backwards in **90-minute intervals** (typically 5 or 6 cycles, equivalent to 7.5 or 9 hours).
3. Subtract **15 minutes** for sleep latency (time to fall asleep).
*Example:* To wake up at 7:00 AM:
- 5 cycles backward = 11:30 PM
- Minus 15 mins = Bedtime of **11:15 PM**.

### Why do I wake up tired after 8 hours of sleep?
Sleeping exactly 8 hours can sometimes force you to wake up in the middle of a deep sleep stage (which occurs in 90-minute waves). Waking up at 7.5 hours (5 full cycles) or 9 hours (6 full cycles) is often more effective than exactly 8 hours because it aligns with cycle completions.

### How do shift workers optimize daytime sleep?
- Use the **Shift Work Sleep Calculator** to split sleep or block continuous hours.
- Utilize blackout curtains and keep a cool bedroom temperature (65°F / 18.3°C).
- Block blue light 2 hours before daytime bedtime.
