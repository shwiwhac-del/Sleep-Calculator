## 🧮 Core Algorithms & Scientific Logic

### 1. Sleep Cycle Math (The 90-Minute Rule)
- A complete sleep cycle is assumed to average **90 minutes** (1.5 hours) for adults.
- **Fall Asleep Offset**: An average of **15 minutes** is added to bedtime choices to account for sleep latency (time required to fall asleep).
- **Formulas**:
  - **Wake-up target calculated from strict bedtime**:
    $$\text{WakeUp} = \text{Bedtime} + \text{SleepLatency (15m)} + (N \times 90 \text{ minutes})$$ where $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ (representing sleep cycle counts).
  - **Bedtime target calculated from strict wake-up time**:
    $$\text{Bedtime} = \text{WakeUp} - \text{SleepLatency (15m)} - (N \times 90 \text{ minutes})$$
- Optimal sleep recommendations highlight **5 cycles (7.5 hours of solid sleep)** and **6 cycles (9.0 hours of solid sleep)** as the standard healthy range.

### 2. Nap Calculator Presets
- **Power Nap (20 Minutes)**: Boosts alertness and performance without entering deep sleep (no sleep inertia).
- **Cat Nap (30 Minutes)**: Good for relaxation, but risks mild grogginess.
- **Restorative Nap (60 Minutes)**: Deep slow-wave sleep. Best for memory consolidation/recall, but induces temporary post-nap sleep inertia.
- **Recovery Nap (90 Minutes)**: Full sleep cycle including REM. Eliminates morning or midday sleep debt, avoids grogginess by waking up at the cycle's biological boundary.

### 3. Sleep Requirements by Age
- **Infants (4-12 months)**: 12 to 16 hours (including naps)
- **Toddlers (1-2 years)**: 11 to 14 hours
- **Preschoolers (3-5 years)**: 10 to 13 hours
- **School Age (6-12 years)**: 9 to 12 hours
- **Teenagers (13-18 years)**: 8 to 10 hours
- **Adults (18-64 years)**: 7 to 9 hours
- **Older Adults (65+ years)**: 7 to 8 hours

## 📂 Web Site & Page Routing Schema
The application structure is fully responsive, accessible, and provides client paths rendered as specific layouts under `/src/pages`:
1. `/` (Home View) — The primary interaction console containing Bedtime inputs, Wake-up targets, Sleep Cycle visual charts, the Nap Calculator, and the Sleep logs journal database toggle.
2. `/about` (About Page) — High-level scientific backing on why natural sleep tracking works, company objectives, and human-first sleep hygiene rules.
3. `/blog` (Guides & FAQ Catalog) — Highly detailed internal resource sheets indexed properly regarding REM sleep, circardian rhythms, sleep debt, and night shifts.
4. `/contact` (Contact) — Standard inquiries/feedback portals for app support.
5. `/privacy` (Privacy Policy) — Strict privacy guarantees (All data stays local on the client's web storage; no personal profiles are ever tracked or aggregated).
6. `/terms` (Terms of Services) — General terms of usage.
7. `/404` (NotFound) — Graceful redirect path for invalid routes.

## 🗃️ Codebase Layout & Critical Components

### Core Components (`/src/components`)
- **`SleepGuideAndFAQ.tsx`**: Renders comprehensive user-friendly guides right under the main calculator, detailing sleep latency, why 90-minute sleep increments matter, sleep by age parameters, and sleep hygiene instructions.
- **`SleepCycleChart.tsx`**: Provides deep visual intuition of light sleep, deep sleep, and REM phases on a custom coordinate system/recharts block helping users visualise physical phases.
- **TimePicker.tsx**: Highly accessible, mobile-responsive custom slider or selection wheel for configuring perfect wake up hours.
- **`SleepJournal.tsx`**: Quick client-side logger allowing users to write down nightly ratings, wake energy levels, bedtimes, and monitor sleep quality streaks over time.
- **`StarryBackground.tsx`**: Dynamic styled backdrop creating an immersive starry night experience using hardware-accelerated animated canvases or SVG points.
- **`AutoLinker.tsx`**: Internal custom linker matching critical keywords automatically into internal informational pages to avoid manual routing configuration.
- **`FeedbackModal.tsx`**: Collects subjective ratings of sleep schedule recommendations with delightful animations.
